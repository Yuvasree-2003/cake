Frequently Asked Questions

How many days before should I book?

We recommend placing orders at least 2-3 days in advance. For elaborate custom or wedding cakes, please reach out 1-2 weeks prior to ensure availability.

Do you make eggless cakes?

Yes, absolutely! We offer delicious eggless options for almost all of our cake flavors upon request.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we provide safe and secure delivery within Chennai. Delivery charges apply based on the distance from our location in East Tambaram.

Can I customize my cake?

We love creating custom designs! Please use our 'Customize Your Cake' form to share your ideas, reference images, and requirements.
